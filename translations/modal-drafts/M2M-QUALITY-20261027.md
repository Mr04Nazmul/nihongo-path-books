# M2M100 Japanese–Bengali benchmark, 2026-10-27

Eight hand-selected difficult Japanese sentences were translated on Modal with `facebook/m2m100_1.2B`; this is not a random sample or a corpus-wide assessment. The workflow completed successfully, but output is unreviewed and unsuitable for automatic import.

Examples of material problems: 2 omits brushing the dust away; 3 mistranslates mismatched socks; 4 loses “beside”; 5 changes the speaker; 6 changes the tournament into a week; 7 changes holding the tree to standing on it. The first output changes the prohibition against talking with a full mouth, and the eighth changes wondering whether a party will occur into hoping it will.

This benchmark changed no corpus entries or review status. The benchmark app stopped after completion, and automatic workflow execution was disabled. The workflow's success means inference ran, not that translations passed quality review.
