# Modal GPU draft quality checkpoint — 2026-10-27

The first Qwen pilot produced two malformed Bengali examples and was rejected.
The MADLAD-400-3B-MT pilot produced two plausible examples, followed by a
100-example batch. All 102 MADLAD outputs are unreviewed machine drafts.
No model output was imported into the book corpus or review ledger.

All 100 batch records have distinct keys and source and previous-translation
hashes matched the local corpus at inspection time. The automatic script checks
returned no flags; this does not establish translation correctness.

A non-random, manually selected sample of 11 from the 100 contained at least
eight meaning errors or material omissions, including:

- 靴のほこりをはけではらいなさい。: brushing dust off shoes was rendered
  as hanging up a shoe-related item.
- 車のわきの男の子をごらんなさい。: beside the car became sitting behind it.
- この木にしっかりつかまりなさい。: holding onto the tree became standing on it.
- 今晩はパーティーがあるのかなあ。: uncertainty was omitted.

These examples demonstrate that presence-of-Bengali and script checks are
insufficient. The sample is not a statistical estimate of corpus accuracy.
All 100 batch records remain unresolved; semantic QA, correction, hash checks
and review evidence are required before display. No human review is claimed.
Automatic PR-triggered GPU runs are paused until a stronger validation method
is evaluated. Source IDs and Japanese were not changed.
